# Brand — Zikkaron

## Name

- **Zikkaron** — Hebrew זִכָּרוֹן — memorial, remembrance, lasting record  
- Tone: civic, professional, cooperative with government  
- **Not** vigilante. **Not** a fake .gov site  
- Avoid sacred / Holocaust overtones  

## Tagline

*A memorial layer that works with authorities — not instead of them.*

## Subtitle

*Memorial records assisting owners and government authorities*

## Voice

- “assist authorities,” “case support pack,” “authorized occupancy verification”
- Not “we arrest squatters”
- Always clarify sovereignty of county / LE / courts

## Visual

- Civic / archival — cool stone papers, ink typography, restrained teal accent  
- No official seals, badges, or “FBI verified” chrome  
- Persistent dark banner with partnership + non-government disclaimer  

## Footer

*Zikkaron* (Hebrew: memorial) — civic evidence layer assisting owners and authorities. Not title. Not force. Not a government website.
